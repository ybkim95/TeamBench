                             KEY FINDING
================================================================================

[OBJECTIVE] Assess whether team coordination (planner + executor + verifier)
is cost-efficient compared to single-agent baselines.

[FINDING] Team coordination is NOT cost-efficient when measured as score-per-
dollar spent. Cost increases (4.71x) exceed score gains (1.97x), resulting in
75% loss of cost-efficiency (0.25x ratio).

[FINDING] HOWEVER, team coordination provides meaningful accuracy improvement
(+19.7%) that may justify the cost for accuracy-critical applications.

[STAT:effect_size] Oracle cost-efficiency: 183.22 score/$
                  Full team cost-efficiency: 46.54 score/$
                  Ratio: 0.25x (team is 4x less efficient per dollar)

[STAT:effect_size] Oracle avg score: 0.6754
                  Full team avg score: 0.8085
                  Improvement: +0.1332 (+19.7%)

[LIMITATION] Token estimates assume 150 tokens/sec throughput (conservative
lower bound). Pricing based on Gemini 3 Flash list rates without volume
discounts. Single-model analysis; results may not generalize to other models
or math-heavy tasks.

================================================================================
                      TOKEN EFFICIENCY ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Cost per token:     ~$0.00019 (constant across all conditions)

Score per 1M tokens (Token Efficiency):

  Restricted:       40.55 score/1M tokens  (most efficient)
  Oracle:           34.81 score/1M tokens
  Team (no-plan):   19.37 score/1M tokens
  Team (no-verify): 16.65 score/1M tokens
  Full:              8.84 score/1M tokens  (least efficient)

Why does token efficiency decline with team complexity?
  → Team agents communicate more (more tokens)
  → Each additional token produces diminishing returns on score
  → Example: 72K additional tokens only add 0.1332 score points
  → Result: 75% efficiency loss despite 20% score gain

Implication: Token optimization (compression, summarization) could improve
team cost-efficiency substantially.
