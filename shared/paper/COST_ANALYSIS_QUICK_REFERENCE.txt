Why does Full Team have worse score-per-token?
  Because team agents produce MORE tokens but with DIMINISHING RETURNS

    Oracle:     19,400 tokens → 0.6754 score = 34.81 score/1M tokens

  So efficiency drops by 75% even though score improves by 20%.
